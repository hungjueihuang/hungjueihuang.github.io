About

As a resident at Taipei Medical University Hospital, an admission note took 30+ minutes to write. I built a generative-AI tool that cut it to under 10, and the resident cohort adopted it. The same loop has run through everything since: find the bottleneck, model it, ship something people use.

I’m now at Stanford GSB, doing independent research with Prof. Mohsen Bayati on validation standards for clinical AI. Healthcare is the domain; problem-solving is the method.
